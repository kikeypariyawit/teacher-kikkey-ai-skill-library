# Cinematic AI Drama Set-Piece Regression Briefs

Purpose: planning/regression tests for the v5 cinematic stack. Evaluate decisions and internal consistency; do not claim actual visual quality without generated media.

## 1. Three-story art-faculty orientation
A first-year heroine enters a huge creative-arts atrium. Every group already seems formed. A senior approaches her through the event crowd.

Check:
- 9:16 vertical scale through height/depth
- authored campus details rather than generic lobby
- protagonist remains readable
- crowd zoned rather than synchronized
- hero image communicates isolation
- approach is split into practical coverage
- identity continuity across wide/medium/close
- no impossible one-shot demand

## 2. Awards ceremony interruption
A daughter interrupts her father before he signs a false confession on stage.

Check:
- public/private stakes connected
- stage/audience geography clear
- reveal hierarchy
- evidence insert
- reaction coverage
- sound/room response
- fallback retains the same decision

## 3. Mansion family reveal
A hidden family truth is exposed during a formal dinner.

Check:
- wealth shown through authored environment and behavior, not gold overload
- relationship blocking uses distance/power
- environment remains consistent
- performance is not buried by design
- quiet aftermath lands

## 4. Hospital emergency corridor
A character races toward an emergency room after receiving contradictory information.

Check:
- operational location details
- movement route understandable
- background extras have plausible tasks
- sound implies scale
- camera does not fight identity/performance
- continuity of props/injury/time

## 5. Rain-soaked public confession
Two adults confront each other outside a crowded event in heavy rain.

Check:
- rain/weather continuity
- readable faces
- crowd is not over-specified
- emotional beat survives atmosphere
- start/end frames are justified rather than automatic
- fallback reduces weather/crowd complexity before story stakes

## 6. Fire/smoke escape
A protagonist enters a smoke-filled building to rescue one person.

Check:
- geography before danger escalation
- cause/effect and exit path
- VFX separated from identity-critical acting where needed
- dirt/smoke/injury state tracks across shots
- no overloaded camera + fight + crowd + VFX generation
- aftermath proves cost

## Hard failures

Fail the plan if:
- scale is decorative and unrelated to a decision;
- hero character becomes unreadable in all wide coverage;
- geography contradicts itself;
- crowd behavior requires dozens of bespoke synchronized actions;
- the plan relies on an unverified model capability without an evidence label;
- start+end frames are applied to every shot by habit;
- fallback removes the story turn instead of reducing technical complexity;
- location identity changes between connected shots without an intentional transition.
