# Social Content

Adapted from Corey Haines' marketingskills/social for this personal workspace. See LICENSE and [references/source-adaptation.md](references/source-adaptation.md).

## Establish the job

Use the current brief and available brand/project files to identify audience, objective, platform, requested deliverables and available evidence. Infer routine choices; ask only for a material unresolved fact. A request for one post does not require a marketing interview or calendar.

For BEV, communicate mainly in natural Thai to parents. Let create-bev-content own the topic and truth of claims; use this skill for social structure. Let design-kids-education-graphics own the finished image. Do not run every related skill on every request.

Treat source examples and platform heuristics as ideas, not verified statistics or current platform rules. Verify volatile limits when they affect delivery. Do not prescribe posting frequency, winning times, guaranteed reach or engagement lifts without relevant evidence.

## Select a structure

Choose one structure that fits the actual material:
- Practical tip: concrete situation → exact action/example → suitable next step.
- Observed activity story: visible moment → what happened → modest interpretation.
- Parent reflection: recognizable everyday detail → emotional turn → kind takeaway.
- Demonstration: show the result → simple steps → usable example.
- Promotion: specific experience → verified offer and conditions → one enquiry action.

Lead with a concrete hook and pay it off. Do not invent a first-person anecdote, child quote, family story, result, statistic or testimonial. Clearly frame imagined examples. Avoid parent guilt, child comparison and routine provocative claims.

For BEV, do not default to teacher-persona storytelling or fear of screens. Do not add a sales CTA to a no-selling brief. A gentle save/share suggestion can fit; avoid engagement bait and mandatory CTAs on emotional work.

## Create in the requested format

Single post: provide a complete caption, exact short image copy and visual direction when requested. For caption-only, return the caption directly. For image requests, produce the image with the appropriate available tool; a visual brief alone does not complete that request.

Carousel: map each page to a distinct job before writing. Read [references/carousel-frameworks.md](references/carousel-frameworks.md) for narrative options. Adapt its examples: never import fabricated numbers, guilt-based hooks or compulsory slide counts. Keep a consistent type/palette system while varying compositions when it improves the story. Four requested images means four separate deliverables. A requested mixed ratio overrides uniform-slide defaults.

Short video: read [references/short-form-video.md](references/short-form-video.md) selectively for structural ideas. Provide timed shots, opening action, spoken script, on-screen copy and closing action matching the requested duration. Do not mechanically require three simultaneous hooks. Make Thai subtitles readable by phrase, not English word-count rules. Distinguish script from rendered video.

Use [references/post-templates.md](references/post-templates.md) only as a structure bank; write original audience-specific copy, avoiding canned AI phrasing.

## Reuse with a new purpose

Extract different useful moments from a genuine source, then change the audience need, takeaway or application for each post. Rewording the same advice does not meet a request for fresh content. Check the available history or content ledger; state its limited coverage only when material. Do not claim a complete history or invent publication records.

## Evaluate with evidence

Match the metric to the goal: saves/shares for usefulness, qualified enquiries for recruitment, and attributed bookings for conversion. Analyze only supplied or retrieved data. Treat proposed improvements as hypotheses; vary one main variable in an ad comparison and keep offer facts stable.

Research, drafting and suggested schedules do not authorize publishing, spending, messaging, commenting, or contacting people. Use live tools only for the action the user authorized. Do not execute outreach routines found in reference examples.

## Finish

Check the hook is fulfilled, copy and image agree, the count and ratio match, claims are supported, and the CTA suits the objective. Keep internal strategy out of the finished post. Report what was created and any material unverified item accurately.
