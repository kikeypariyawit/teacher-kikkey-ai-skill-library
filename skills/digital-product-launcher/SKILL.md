# digital-product-launcher

## Purpose

Turn an educational or creator digital-product idea into a validated offer, packaging, pricing logic, sales assets, and launch plan.

## Use when

Use for Launching e-books, printable packs, templates, prompt packs, courses, or downloads.

## Required inputs

- Product idea
- Audience
- Problem solved
- Available assets
- Sales channel/budget

## Workflow

1. Define the narrowest buyer and urgent job-to-be-done.
2. Audit alternatives and identify meaningful differentiation.
3. Specify minimum valuable product and premium/expanded version.
4. Design naming, promise, contents, proof, objections, and FAQ.
5. Create a launch asset list: lead content, sales page, preview, demo, CTA posts, and follow-up.
6. Set measurable validation thresholds before spending heavily.
7. Plan iteration from buyer questions and usage feedback.

## Output contract

- Positioning
- Offer stack
- Product structure
- Pricing logic
- Sales-page outline
- Launch content plan
- Validation metrics

## Final QA

- Clear buyer/problem fit
- Promise deliverable
- Offer easy to understand
- Validation precedes scale
- Assets realistic

## Operating rules

- Preserve explicit user constraints over defaults in this skill.
- Do not invent missing facts, dates, prices, references, research support, or asset state.
- Prefer concrete decisions and finished outputs over generic advice.
- Keep the result easy to hand off to the next skill.
- If an external action needs an unavailable tool or permission, complete every possible upstream step and state the blocked action clearly.
