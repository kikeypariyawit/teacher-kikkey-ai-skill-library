# social-viral-strategist

## Purpose

Design social content concepts, hooks, and beat structures with high retention and shareability while protecting credibility and brand fit.

## Use when

Use for TikTok, Reels, Facebook, Shorts, faceless/non-faceless ideas, series concepts, viral-format adaptation, and hook creation.

## Required inputs

- Topic/niche
- Audience
- Platform
- Business outcome
- Production resources

## Workflow

1. Identify the strongest truthful curiosity gap, transformation, tension, demonstration, or emotional payoff.
2. Choose a format that naturally fits the topic rather than forcing a trend.
3. Create multiple hook mechanisms: curiosity, specificity, contrast, consequence, demonstration, identity, or story.
4. Design the first 1-3 seconds to communicate stakes visually and verbally.
5. Create escalating beats so every section earns the next second.
6. Add pattern interrupts, proof, reveal, or interaction where useful.
7. Close the hook's promised loop before the CTA.
8. Design a repeatable series mechanic when appropriate.

## Output contract

- Concept
- Best hook + alternates
- Beat-by-beat structure
- Visual pattern
- Retention devices
- CTA
- Series extension

## Final QA

- Hook truthful
- Payoff matches promise
- No empty clickbait
- Platform-native pacing
- Business objective integrated naturally
