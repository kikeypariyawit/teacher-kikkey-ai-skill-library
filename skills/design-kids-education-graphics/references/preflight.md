# Graphic preflight

Review the actual artifact, not just the intended prompt. For small edits, inspect the change plus nearby content and preservation requirements; do not rerun irrelevant checks.

## Required correctness

- Copy: compare Thai letters and marks, English, punctuation, prices, dates, hours, and contacts against the text inventory and latest brief. Watch for repeated or omitted words.
- Brand: use the correct supplied logo, once as intended, undistorted and unaltered. A similar-looking generated mark does not pass an exact-asset requirement.
- Photo: compare source and result for face, expression, hands, clothing, equipment, and setting. Claim unchanged pixels only when original layers and the production method substantiate it.
- Facts: no inherited outdated offer, invented scarcity, false testimonial, or unsupported learning guarantee. Show material offer conditions legibly.
- Privacy and safety: no unnecessary child identifiers; no misleading alteration or newly invented unsafe activity. Apply existing authorization to the draft and resolve actual concerns.
- Format: verify actual dimensions and requested orientation, count, and file separation. Do not infer the output ratio from the prompt alone when dimensions are accessible.

Any material correctness failure needs repair or an explicit limitation before handoff.

## Full-size inspection

Read every text block. Check Thai mark clearance, edges, masks, source crop, image sharpness, natural skin tones, illustration anatomy, speech tails, alignment, and visual seams. For narrow edits, compare unchanged areas with the accepted version.

## Phone-size inspection

Inspect at approximately 360 px display width when available. Can the viewer read the headline without zooming, identify the subject and action, then locate the key supporting detail? Is there a dominant element? Do details and logo still have breathing room? Keep faces, working hands, and required conditions clear.

Safe margins reduce cropping risk but cannot guarantee every platform preview. Do not claim verified feed behavior without seeing the relevant preview.

## Design diagnosis

Assess message clarity, composition, type, color/light, and relevance to the brief separately. Use concrete observations, such as “the price competes with three badges,” rather than unsupported 10/10 scores. A beautiful illustration cannot compensate for the wrong requested medium, and accurate text cannot compensate for unreadability.

## Series checks

Count separate outputs; verify order, per-image ratio, distinct content, shared brand treatment, and any required edge continuity. A collage of four panels does not satisfy four separate image files.

## Repair and stop

Repair the most consequential defect with a localized change when possible. Reinspect the changed area and adjacent content. Stop once the requested output is correct, readable, and visually coherent. If inspection or a necessary fix is unavailable, state exactly what remains unverified; do not claim preflight passed.
