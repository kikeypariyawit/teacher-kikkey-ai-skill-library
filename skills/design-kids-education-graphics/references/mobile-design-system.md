# Mobile-first design system

Use these defaults only when the user or brand does not provide a stricter system.

## Canvas and safe areas

| Placement | Canvas | Practical content zone |
|---|---:|---|
| Facebook/Instagram feed | 1080 x 1350 (4:5) | Keep essential content about 72 px from the sides and 96 px from top/bottom |
| Story/Reels/TikTok | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | Keep essential content about 80 px from the sides, 180 px from the top, and 300 px from the bottom |
| Portrait requested at 3:4 | 1080 x 1440 | Recompose with similar proportional margins; do not stretch 4:5 artwork |
| Square reuse | 1080 x 1080 (1:1) | Design from the central square rather than cropping faces or text afterward |

Platform UI and previews vary. Keep the hook, face, and CTA closer to the visual center than the minimum margins suggest.

## Type hierarchy

Use Thai-capable fonts with clear tone marks, such as Prompt, Noto Sans Thai, or Sarabun, when available. Prefer one or two compatible font families and a small weight range. Verify that the chosen font supports Thai in the actual renderer; a declared font name does not prove it loaded.

| Role | 1080 px-wide starting point | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Main hook | 64–92 px | 1–3 short lines; emphasize one phrase only |
| Supporting line | 36–48 px | Keep it brief and high contrast |
| Details | 30–38 px | Use cards or a carousel when details exceed 5–7 short lines |
| CTA | 34–44 px | One direct action |
| Conditions | 26–30 px minimum | Never hide material conditions in unreadably small type |

Adjust for the font's actual x-height and Thai mark clearance. Avoid tight line spacing that clips vowels or tone marks. Break Thai lines at meaningful phrase boundaries; do not space individual Thai letters to imitate Latin tracking. Use strong solid type for long Thai text; reserve outlines, shadows, and display effects for a short accent when they improve readability.

Set headline size from its rendered width, not a fixed maximum. If it becomes too long, shorten the copy with the user's intent intact or split it into balanced phrases. Do not remove conditions or alter approved wording merely to make it fit. Keep a readable separate detail area when exact wording is required.

## Composition

- Make one element dominant: the child/activity image or the headline, not both equally.
- Reserve clean negative space for text before placing decoration.
- Use one alignment system per panel.
- Place text on a solid, gradient, or softly blurred support when the photo is busy.
- Keep overlays clear of eyes, mouths, hands, safety equipment, and the action being demonstrated.
- Use rounded cards and playful shapes sparingly; avoid a preschool look when parents are the decision-makers.

## Color and accessibility

- Use official brand colors when supplied.
- If no palette exists, derive a restrained palette from the authentic photo: one dark neutral, one light neutral, one primary accent, and one secondary accent.
- Maintain strong contrast for all essential copy. Check contrast instead of assuming that shadows or outlines make text readable.
- Do not communicate meaning with color alone.
- Avoid oversaturated greens and artificial skin tones in outdoor BEV imagery.

## BEV visual direction

When the task is for Bangyai English Village, default to warm editorial early learning: real outdoor activity, natural greens and wood, bright but believable daylight, confident candid children, and modern parent-facing typography. Keep the experience adventurous and educational rather than turning the scene into a generic classroom or cartoon playground.

Use the official BEV logo supplied by the user. Do not redraw or approximate it. Keep a clear area around the logo and size it as a brand signature, not as the main message.

## Reformatting and copy density

Ratios describe width:height. Respect explicit orientation; resolve genuinely contradictory requests before choosing dimensions. Default to 4:5 only when no ratio was requested. Keep an existing requested 3:4 or 9:16 format rather than normalizing everything to a default.

A simple feed graphic usually needs a headline, one supporting idea, and a small brand signature. A useful phrase may replace the supporting line. Promotions need their actual date, price, and material conditions; reduce decorative copy first. These are density guidelines, not mandatory field counts.

Rebuild spacing and crop for each format instead of stretching the original or shaving off outer text. Use consistent outer margins and a small spacing rhythm; check Thai ascenders, descenders, and tone marks in the exported result. Keep background artwork full bleed when intended, while protecting required content inside the safe area.
