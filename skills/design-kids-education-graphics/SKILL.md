# Design Kids Education Graphics

Create finished graphics with a clear idea, intentional composition, readable Thai, and trustworthy imagery. Preserve the user's requested subject, medium, source assets, and deliverable count. A polished prompt is not a completed image request.

## Establish the brief and inspect assets

Infer the audience, purpose, message, format, desired action, visual style, and required assets from the current request and available context. For BEV, use `create-bev-content` when available and new copy or factual grounding is needed; do not require it for an already specified text edit.

Inspect supplied assets before choosing a direction. Classify each as official logo, authentic activity photo, existing artwork to edit, content reference, or style reference. Do not treat generated children on an old poster as documentary photography, or carry that poster's prices and dates into a new campaign without checking the latest brief.

If a supplied local path fails, check the current attachment mapping and actual files before declaring the image unavailable. Use an available authorized source when appropriate; do not assume access to a previous conversation's files. Ask for reattachment only if the required asset remains unavailable. Never invent a replacement logo or real child.

Make routine design decisions and draft copy without an approval round. Ask only when a material ambiguity cannot be resolved from context, such as contradictory orientation instructions. A supplied photo for a requested design authorizes that draft; do not re-request consent already established. Actual publishing or messaging is a separate action.

## Choose a visual direction

For new images, broad redesigns, or feedback that work looks generic, apply [references/visual-execution.md](references/visual-execution.md): make the compact direction card, choose one composition, then evaluate correctness and visual craft separately on the actual output. Use installed `canvas-design` only when additional composition work is useful; keep this skill as production owner.

For a new design or broad redesign, read [references/art-direction.md](references/art-direction.md). Compare a few meaningfully different compositions internally, select the strongest fit, and proceed. Show options only when requested or when a consequential unresolved choice needs the user.

For **BEV Facebook feed graphics**, also read [references/bev-facebook-style-reference.md](references/bev-facebook-style-reference.md). Treat it as the preferred visual grammar when the request asks for the current BEV look, a Facebook-ready design, or a style similar to the user-approved 2026-09-15 references. Use its four composition families as a repertoire, not fixed templates, and keep platform notes date-sensitive rather than hard-coding them as permanent facts.

Choose photography, illustration, objects, or typography because it serves this specific message. Children's education does not automatically mean cute animal characters. When real activity is central and suitable photos are supplied, prioritize those photos. An editorial illustration can suit a general parenting or language post, but must not impersonate a BEV event or override an explicit real-photo request.

For a narrow edit, preserve the accepted direction. Define what changes and what must remain before editing. Do not turn a price correction into a full redesign.

## Select a feasible production method

Read [references/production-and-revisions.md](references/production-and-revisions.md) for image creation, photo composition, logo insertion, multi-image delivery, or revision work.

Follow the active tool's requirements and the user's tool choice. Use image generation for requested illustration and eligible raster creation/editing. Do not replace requested generated artwork with a basic code-drawn placeholder. Prefer editable typesetting for exact text and unchanged image layers for exact photo/logo preservation when permitted. These preferences do not authorize bypassing a tool restriction.

Short display copy may be generated with artwork when supported; supply exact strings and inspect the actual result. Dense copy, precise offer terms, or required exact brand assets favor a permitted workflow with separate text and original image layers. Never promise pixel identity from generative editing. If exact preservation and available tools conflict, complete the feasible portions and explain the specific remaining limitation instead of silently changing medium or claiming success.

## Compose for the viewer

Read [references/mobile-design-system.md](references/mobile-design-system.md) for layout and resizing. Establish one dominant element, then supporting information, brand, and an action only when appropriate. This is a reading hierarchy, not a fixed top-to-bottom template.

Keep exact copy in a compact text inventory while working. Use natural Thai, meaningful line breaks, and short functional English. Keep text off faces, working hands, and safety equipment. Put explanation in the caption when the image is becoming an essay. Respect a requested single page; summarize secondary information before proposing extra pages.

For learning graphics, match visual language to the age band and task without assuming all older learners need harder vocabulary. For parent-facing posts, keep the composition adult-readable even when the subject is playful. Do not promise virality, guaranteed learning outcomes, or developmental benefits unsupported by evidence.

When a visual includes a developmental, health-adjacent, language-learning, or behavioral claim rather than a simple visible activity description, require claim grounding before locking the on-image copy. Route substantial BEV evidence work through `bev-education-researcher`; put only the safest short takeaway on the image and keep nuance or sources in the caption when useful.

## Protect authenticity and facts

Use the available official logo once per standalone graphic unless a different brand arrangement is requested. Preserve its artwork, proportions, lettering, and clear space. Do not redraw it, fabricate a QR code, or substitute a text label while calling it the official logo.

For authentic photography, preserve identity, expression, skin, clothing, bodies, hands, equipment, and setting. Do not beautify faces or invent child outcomes. Avoid private names and unnecessary identifying details. Resolve a concrete rights concern if present; do not add a hypothetical permission checklist to a routine authorized draft.

Use the latest supplied or verified facts for prices, dates, hours, contacts, and conditions. Check conflicts before using them. A style reference is not a current source of business facts.

## Inspect, repair, and deliver

Read [references/preflight.md](references/preflight.md) before delivery. Inspect the rendered artifact at full size and approximately 360 px display width when accessible. A prompt alone is not visual QA. Fix concrete defects with targeted revisions, then recheck the affected area and any neighboring content. Stop when the requested output meets the brief; do not regenerate repeatedly for speculative improvements.

Deliver the finished image(s), actual format, and matching caption when requested. For a prompt-only request, deliver the prompt and exact copy instead. Keep art-direction rationale, internal checklists, and tool details out of the post and avoid dumping them into the handoff. Report only material limitations. Follow the active environment's saving and display rules; distinguish a generated preview, exported file, editable source, and published post accurately.
