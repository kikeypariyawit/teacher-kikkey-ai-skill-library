# BEV visual direction

## Consistent brand, varied compositions

Use the newest supplied reference and official assets first. Default to a warm, bright, premium early-learning editorial style: natural green, soft cream, restrained mustard or coral accent, and clear Thai sans serif type with enough weight. These are creative defaults, not an official colour specification. Avoid murky green casts, crowded stickers, artificial classroom scenes, and template repetition.

Select a composition that explains the idea:

| Treatment | Good fit | Main constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Real-photo hero with editorial text field | A visible child/activity moment | Keep faces and working hands clear |
| Object-led still life with type | A practical home activity without real photos | Do not imply the generated scene is BEV |
| Two clearly labelled examples | Compare useful phrases or material choices | Avoid shaming “good child / bad child” framing |
| Three-step visual sequence | A small activity or routine | Split into a carousel if labels become cramped |
| One large English phrase and Thai application | Useful functional language | Explain the real context, not just vocabulary |

Use illustration for objects, nature, textures, and instructional symbols when appropriate. Do not fabricate documentary child photos. Do not copy another creator's distinctive layout, characters, or wording; extract high-level principles such as hierarchy, spacing, and colour balance.

## Copy and hierarchy

For a single educational image, aim for one headline (usually one or two short lines) and one short supporting line. A phrase example may replace the supporting line. Put steps and explanation in a caption or carousel. Promotion details can use a separate clear information area when requested.

At 1080 × 1350, start around 56–88 px for the headline, 32–44 px for essential supporting copy, and 60–80 px outer margins; adjust for the Thai font and actual rendered preview. These are starting points, not rigid measurements. Use at most two compatible type families, avoid separating Thai combining marks, and use visible contrast. Inspect at roughly 360 px display width; if essential text requires zooming, simplify or split the content.

Use one official logo with clear space. Never use a generated pseudo-logo or improvised QR code. Add times, prices, dates, and contact blocks only when relevant and verified. Do not repeat all brand information on every educational image.

## Production handoff

Specify purpose, dimensions, exact text blocks, composition, palette, photo source or illustration status, logo asset, and what must remain unchanged. For generation, keep prompts focused on a single layout and readable copy; follow the available tool's current guidance. When exact text or photo pixels are mandatory, choose a permitted workflow capable of meeting them and disclose any capability conflict.

Before presenting a completed visual, inspect the actual output when accessible. Compare every visible Thai/English word and number with the approved copy; check edges, crop, logo, faces, hands, and the phone-size hierarchy. Fix an unreadable or misspelled image rather than relying on a correct caption to compensate. If rendering or inspection is unavailable, label the result as a brief or unverified draft instead of reporting a passed preflight.
