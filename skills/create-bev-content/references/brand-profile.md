# Bangyai English Village brand profile

## Core facts

- Audience: Thai parents of children aged 3–11; children are the learners.
- Positioning: English communication through play, movement, problem-solving, nature, and interaction with friends.
- Language environment: activities are conducted in English.
- Place: opposite Central Westgate in the Bangyai area; use the user's current map link when needed.
- Latest operating days in the supplied conversation history: Saturdays and Sundays. Do not assume public-holiday opening.
- The history includes 2-hour and longer programme options with differing time descriptions. Use the latest verified brief for exact times, duration, breaks, and availability; do not reconstruct a schedule from older examples.
- Supplied parent policy: parents enter free.
- Food and café are available on site.
- Primary contact: `www.facebook.com/bangyaienglishvillage`.

Treat schedules, prices, availability, promotions, and policies as changeable. Verify or attribute them to the user's latest brief before publishing.

## Communication priorities

Emphasize:

- children using English naturally during meaningful activities;
- courage to try, think, speak, move, and collaborate;
- authentic fun rather than staged performance;
- an outdoor, green, active learning environment;
- specific visible moments over generic education claims;
- friendly guidance for parents rather than hard selling.

Avoid:

- centering Teacher Kikkey as the brand hero;
- grammar-heavy or exam-focused positioning;
- claims that play automatically produces a guaranteed outcome;
- dense academic language;
- excessive emojis, hashtags, or sales pressure;
- invented testimonials, enrollment numbers, credentials, or child results.

## Voice and visual direction

Write mainly in natural Thai. Use short, correct English examples when they clarify what children might say. Keep the tone warm, professional, creative, and trustworthy.

Use warm editorial early-learning visuals inspired by modern international preschool campaigns. Favor real photography, natural colors, greenery, candid interaction, friendly but strong Thai sans serif typography, clean hierarchy, and comfortable mobile-safe spacing.
