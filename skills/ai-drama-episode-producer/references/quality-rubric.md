# QA with evidence

Complete creation and repair before reporting a verdict. Scores aid editorial review; they do not measure production budget, guarantee model quality, predict virality or replace inspection.

## Planning gates
Check causality, motivated behavior, seeded reveals, language, scene changes, estimated runtime, exact shot/prompt mapping, supported or explicitly provisional model routing, feasible frame transitions, continuity and a concrete next-episode handoff.

Treat any of these as a hard failure irrespective of score:
- contradicted approved canon;
- missing required shot/frame/video prompt or dangling asset ID;
- incompatible shot states with no bridge;
- misleading claim that a tool supports an unverified control;
- implausible speech/action timing;
- fabricated media, approval, visual review or completed export.

Repair locally when possible. If a critical missing input cannot be inferred, finish unaffected work and name precisely what remains unverified.

## Prestige review
For each dimension record one concrete observation and one necessary repair, if any:
- Story causality and escalation.
- Cast distinction and playable performance.
- Production design and spatial coherence.
- Motivated camera, light and visual progression.
- Edit rhythm and runtime.
- Sound and emotional contrast.
- AI generation feasibility and fallback quality.
- Continuity and asset traceability.
- Packaging/readability for the requested format.

Use 0–4 anchors if a score is helpful: 0 absent/contradictory; 1 generic or materially broken; 2 workable with significant repairs; 3 specific and coherent; 4 exceptionally well integrated. Mark unseen media N/A, never full marks. Explain editorial judgments using shot IDs rather than inflated self-awarded totals.

## Visual and final-cut review
Only for actual supplied/generated media, inspect identity, anatomy, hand contact, costume, prop geometry, eye-lines, background continuity, artifacts, performance and start/end matches. For a video cut also check sync, speaker attribution, pacing, sound levels, music transitions, subtitle spelling/readability and exports. Distinguish verified observations from proposed fixes.

End with repairs applied, remaining evidence limits and one truthful status. Do not restart a working episode to improve a cosmetic score.
