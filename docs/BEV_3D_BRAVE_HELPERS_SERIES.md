## 1. Series promise

Short 3D clips (20–25 s, 9:16) in which two clay-toy kids on a Bang Yai garden island practise **one real life-skill rule** — with the key words in English and the explanation in Thai. Every episode is useful to any Thai family, ends with one at-home action, and shows how BEV takes part.

- **Why 3D clay toys:** the dangerous moment can be shown safely (a teddy “Mr. Bear” is the one who falls in — a practice dummy, never a child at risk); kids recognise the toy style; no real child’s face is used or faked.
- **Why English:** BEV’s environment is English; one big action word per step (SHOUT / THROW / REACH) is easy to chant and remember.
- **Where BEV is part of it:** see the participation list in the research note (§4). Only confirmed actions may be mentioned on screen.
