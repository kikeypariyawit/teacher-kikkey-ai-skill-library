**Evidence status key**

- `do-not-use` — conflicting or unverifiable; keep out of public content.

**Safe on-screen wording:** “ถ้าเพื่อนตกน้ำ อย่ากระโดดลงไปช่วย — ตะโกน โยน ยื่น” · “เด็กเล่นน้ำต้องมีผู้ใหญ่ดูแลใกล้ชิดเสมอ” · footnote “หลักการช่วยคนตกน้ำ อ้างอิงกรมควบคุมโรค”.
**Avoid:** death counts on the clip for young viewers (put them in the parent caption with source), “อันดับ 1”, any claim that BEV teaches swimming/survival swimming, any suggestion a watching child is now “safe around water”.

## 4. How BEV can be part of the solution (all `proposed` until confirmed by the owner)

1. **Safety-first briefing** — a 1-minute “Shout · Throw · Reach” chant before boating/bridge activities, in English with Thai support. Low cost, visible, filmable with consent.
2. **Safety Spot** — a sign plus a throwable float/rope at the water activity area, matching the clip’s three steps.
3. **Free for schools** — the clip and a printable poster/colouring sheet shared without logo-gating for kindergartens and schools in Bang Yai / Nonthaburi.
4. **Local partner** — ask the local health centre / district office about the ผู้ก่อการดี network before saying BEV is part of it.
5. **Family practice CTA** — every episode ends with one at-home action (practise the chant with gestures), not a booking push.
