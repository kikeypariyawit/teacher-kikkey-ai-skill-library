2. Complete one caption, exact on-image copy and a feasible visual. Keep a 3–5, 6–8, or 9–11 age fit and one CTA. Create finished artwork only with verified authentic assets or an unambiguous illustration.
3. Set Notion Draft after copy, Design after artwork, Ready after final QA, and Posted only after confirming a live Facebook URL. Add the URL and performance observations; preserve previous history.
4. Review comparable organic outcomes in Content Performance and, once the separate BEV Meta Ads source is connected, read its paid campaign data without editing ads. Identify what to repeat/avoid with sample sizes and dates. Never claim paid-ad performance explains organic results without evidence.
5. Commit substantive weekly calendar revisions here. Keep daily live status and per-post metrics in Notion.
