# THE GLASS HOUSE REMEMBERS — CHARACTER BIBLE

Status: DRAFT V1
Identity rule: recurring characters must use approved master references once generated. Do not redesign faces, age, signature hair or body proportions shot-by-shot.

## MARA VALE — protagonist
Age: 27
Role: art conservator / restoration specialist
Story function: truth-seeker whose identity becomes the mystery

### External presence
- Intelligent, self-contained, observant.
- Slim athletic build; practical movement rather than fashion-model posing.
- Dark brown shoulder-length hair, naturally textured, often tucked behind one ear while working.
- Hazel-green eyes with a steady gaze that becomes visibly unsettled before she admits fear.
- Crescent-shaped birthmark beneath the left collarbone.
- Wardrobe: charcoal, cream, olive, muted blue; work shirts, fitted trousers, soft knit layers, restoration gloves.
- No glamorous makeover unless story-motivated.

### Inner engine
Goal: finish the commission, clear her late mother’s debts, and protect her sense of self.
Wound: her mother died without explaining large gaps in their family history.
Fear: that her life is someone else’s unfinished story.
Secret: since childhood she has dreamed the same sentence—“Don’t let them close the glass.” She has never told anyone.
Contradiction: she distrusts superstition but is deeply affected by sensory memory.
Strength: pattern recognition, patience, moral courage.
Flaw: when frightened, she withholds information to regain control.

### Performance grammar
Baseline: contained breath, economical gestures, investigative gaze.
Under pressure: jaw tightens first; hands remain controlled until emotion breaks through.
High-emotion mode: not constant screaming—use sudden loss of containment after long restraint.

### Arc
“I need facts to know who I am” → “I choose who I am even when the facts remain incomplete.”

## ADRIAN BLACKTHORN — male lead
Age: 31
Role: current owner of Blackthorn House; architectural historian / family heir
Story function: ally, gatekeeper, romantic danger

### External presence
- Tall, lean, composed; understated old-money wardrobe without flashy logos.
- Black-brown hair, slightly long at the front, deliberate but not over-styled.
- Grey eyes; calm face that becomes readable mainly through gaze and breath.
- Wardrobe: black, deep navy, stone, white shirts, long coats; sleeves rolled when working with archives.

### Inner engine
Goal: uncover what truly happened in the 1975 fire without repeating his father’s obsession.
Wound: raised inside a family that treated affection as leverage and secrets as inheritance.
Fear: becoming the next Blackthorn man to use a person as evidence.
Secret: he has possessed a damaged photograph showing Mara’s face since he was sixteen.
Contradiction: he protects people by controlling information, which makes him look manipulative.
Strength: disciplined, loyal under pressure, willing to absorb blame.
Flaw: decides what others are “ready” to know.

### Performance grammar
Baseline: stillness, controlled distance, listens before answering.
Attraction: eye contact held half a beat too long; protective body placement before verbal admission.
Fear: becomes more formal and emotionally colder.
Break point: voice drops rather than rises.

### Arc
“Protection requires control” → “Love requires informed choice.”

## MRS. ELEANOR ROWAN — housekeeper / witness
Age: 68
Role: long-serving steward of Blackthorn House
Story function: ambiguous guardian of the past

### External presence
- Silver hair in a low twist, severe but elegant posture.
- Dark practical dresses, keys at waist, immaculate hands.
- Moves quietly and seems to know where everyone is without asking.

### Inner engine
Goal: complete a promise made to Evelyn in 1975.
Wound: survived the fire and believes survival created a debt.
Secret: she was a teenage assistant in the conservatory the night Evelyn disappeared.
Contradiction: her tenderness toward Mara makes her secrecy more disturbing.

### Performance grammar
Never play as a generic creepy housekeeper. She is warm when warmth is true, frightening when certainty replaces explanation.

## SEBASTIAN BLACKTHORN — season antagonist
Age: 63
Role: Adrian’s estranged uncle; executor of the family trust
Story function: active human pressure against investigation

### External presence
- Precise tailoring, silver hair, measured voice, immaculate manners.
- Carries a cane due to an old fire injury; never played as physically helpless.

### Inner engine
Goal: destroy the portrait and permanently seal the East Wing.
Worldview: not every truth is morally entitled to survive.
Private cost: he was 12 during the 1975 fire and saw something that shattered his family.
Secret: he knows Evelyn deliberately left the portrait where a future conservator would find it.
Strength: strategic, emotionally perceptive, patient.
Flaw: confuses secrecy with mercy.

## EVELYN BLACKTHORN — the woman in the portrait
Age at disappearance: 27
Role: missing/dead woman from 1975; visual double of Mara but not automatically the same person
Story function: central mystery and mirror identity

### Visual rule
Once Mara’s master face is approved, Evelyn should share unmistakable structural resemblance but must not be generated as a literal duplicate in every detail. Differences:
- 1970s hair styling and wardrobe.
- Slightly different brow grooming, posture and expression.
- Evelyn carries more social polish and visible emotional fatigue.

### Inner engine revealed gradually
Goal in 1975: stop the Blackthorn memory experiment from becoming inheritance technology.
Secret: she participated voluntarily before turning against it.

## RELATIONSHIP MATRIX

### Mara ↔ Adrian
EP1: suspicion + involuntary recognition.
EP2: confrontation over hidden knowledge.
EP3: reluctant alliance.
EP4: first voluntary vulnerability.
EP5: intimacy complicated by evidence Adrian withheld.
EP6–7: partnership under threat.
EP8: deep trust just before fracture.
EP9: perceived betrayal.
EP10: informed choice to stand together without surrendering autonomy.

### Mara ↔ Mrs. Rowan
Feels maternally familiar before trust is earned. Rowan’s affection creates pressure rather than comfort.

### Adrian ↔ Sebastian
Family loyalty corroded by incompatible ideas of protection.

### Mara ↔ Evelyn
Not a standard past-life answer by default. The relationship can be biological, technological, psychological and symbolic at different stages until the finale resolves the central truth.

## CAST CONSISTENCY RULES
- Do not make Mara and Evelyn interchangeable in wardrobe or body language.
- Adrian must remain emotionally restrained enough that small changes matter.
- Sebastian should feel credible, not cartoon-villainous.
- Mrs. Rowan is a witness with agency, not a mystery exposition machine.
- Every recurring character gets a Character Master and Look Master before multi-episode generation.
