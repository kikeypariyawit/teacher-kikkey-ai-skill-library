## Reference binding rules
- Character Master owns stable face/body identity.
- Look Master owns wardrobe, hair and makeup state.
- Location Master owns architecture/material language.
- Scene Anchor owns local spatial geometry and light direction.
- Hero Prop Master owns prop identity/state.
- Previous accepted frame may guide local continuity but must not replace the global owner references.

## Approval rule
Do not mark assets APPROVED merely because they were generated. Approval requires explicit user acceptance or a clearly accepted downstream use.
