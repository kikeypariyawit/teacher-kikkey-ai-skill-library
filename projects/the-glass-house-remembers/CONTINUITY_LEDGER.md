# THE GLASS HOUSE REMEMBERS — CONTINUITY LEDGER

## Canon baseline
Story present: 2026.
Mara Vale: age 27, born 1999.
Evelyn Blackthorn: born 1948; disappeared / officially died October 17, 1975, age 27.
The portrait: painted summer 1975, damaged by smoke, later hidden and now commissioned for restoration.
Blackthorn House: active residence owned by Adrian Blackthorn.

## Identity invariants
- Mara: hazel-green eyes, dark brown shoulder-length textured hair, crescent birthmark beneath left collarbone.
- Evelyn: unmistakable structural resemblance to Mara but 1970s styling, posture and expression remain distinct.
- Adrian: grey eyes, black-brown hair, restrained old-money styling.
- Mrs. Rowan: silver hair low twist, elegant severe posture, keys at waist.
- Sebastian: silver hair, precise tailoring, cane from old fire injury.

## EP01 inherited state
- Mara believes the job is an unusual but ordinary private restoration commission.
- Mara has never seen Evelyn’s portrait.
- Adrian has seen the portrait and knows its resemblance to Mara.
- Mara does not know the extent of Adrian’s prior knowledge.
- Mrs. Rowan has not met Mara in person before the commission.
- Mara’s recurring dream phrase “Don’t let them close the glass” remains private.

## EP01 end state
- Mara has seen Evelyn’s face.
- Mara knows the portrait dates to 1975.
- Mara knows Evelyn’s official lifespan is 1948–1975.
- Mara has stated she was born in 1999.
- Mara suspects Adrian intentionally connected her to the portrait.
- Mrs. Rowan reflexively addressed Mara as “Miss Evelyn,” then corrected to “Miss Vale.”
- White camellias are present in the final beat.
- Portrait is only partially cleaned; do not show the hidden birthmark yet.

## EP02 required pickups

- The portrait’s crescent birthmark is the central reveal.
- Do not resolve why Mara resembles Evelyn.
- Adrian may reveal partial truth but must still withhold the old photograph until a later planned beat.
