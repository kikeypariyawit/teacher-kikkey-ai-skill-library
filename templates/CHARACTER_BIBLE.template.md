## Operating rules
- Never infer approval from a generated variation.
- A visually attractive generation that breaks approved identity is still a continuity failure.
- Character growth may change behavior, wardrobe, hair, or confidence only when supported by story or explicit approval.
- Before a new episode, carry the latest approved state into `CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md` and the episode production package.
